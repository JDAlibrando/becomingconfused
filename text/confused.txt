A Memory In A Dream
In my dreams
I remember a place
a clearing
At the end of a trail
in a mountain pass

In my dreams
I am searching for that memory
With desire for wholeness
For what peace once was
I hope for that clearing
and its satisfaction
and fear that I will forgot
and am too lost to return

In my dreams
I will become confused
wandering in promises
my thoughts striving 
after phantasmagoria
to other clearings
mere afterimages
burned into my retina
by my own imaginings

So my companions
desire, hope, fear, and confusion
encourage my journey
through ever shifting form
a tectonic subduction zone
disguising the same earth

Of course that memory 
is a feeling
and is not in that landscape

Do not despair my dream
but your comedy is on repeat
Oh if my wakeful mind 
could ease you
of your confused neurosis

And that I might learn from you
What happened,
between that soup of amino acids
primordial slime
lobe finned fish
and now,
why thoughts began striving
after phantasmagoria
