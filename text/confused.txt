A Memory in a Dream of a Dream
In my dreams
I remember a clearing 
at the end of a trail
in a mountain pass.

In my dreams
I am searching for that memory.
With desire for wholeness
I have hope that i'll reach that clearing
and be satisfied by it.
With hope for success
I have fear that I will loose my way
too lost to return.

In my dreams
I become confused
wandering in hope 
my thoughts striving after phantasmagoria
behind each crest another summit
with teasing afterimages
burned into my retina
by my own imaginings.

So my companions
desire, hope, fear, and confusion
encourage my progression
through shifting terrain
a tectonic subduction zone
disguising the same earth.

But that memory 
is only a feeling
and does not exist in that landscape.

Do not despair my dream
but your comedy is on repeat.
Oh if my wakeful mind 
could ease you
of your confused neurosis.

And that I might learn from you
what happened between that 
soup of amino acids, 
primordial slime, 
lobe finned fish, 
and now
so that thoughts began striving 
after phantasmagoria.
