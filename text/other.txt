Who We Are
Maybe we must be who we are
Those patterns that are the source of shame and pride
Those patterns that ensnare and empower
Without those patterns, we are not or all, because
Patterns distinguish us
And freedom is a death
As plucking a flower frees it from becoming a fruit
Change is also a death
As fruit replaces flower replaces bud
Maybe there is no hope that we won't be ourselves
A fitting-seamlessly into that tapestry-like song
That content and cause are the same is a whisper
Yelling, “You don’t control, but are control!”
Even acceptance of this comes from outside
And faith in the outside comes from the outside
As The Other comes from that unknown water
A deep, dark water on the surface
Symbols from the unconscious and forms from the world
Thoughts from the ego and affect from the body
It seems we have no choice but to be what we are
No choice but to choose when decisions happen
No chance but to try when effort comes
And we must wait for patience 
whether or not we are patient for it, because
We are what we can’t have
The Other, as it passes into memory
