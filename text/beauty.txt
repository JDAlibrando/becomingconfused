That Echoing Undertone
Beauty has a horror,
an echoing undertone,
reverberating sympathetically by some distant clarity
that leaves tinnitus in lowly ears at odd moments.

A message that meaning,
is a flame to a moth,
beyond the arena of this personality.

A message that I must love
by opening to this lowliness.
Compassion that he must only know echoes.
Sympathies resonating in what is heavy
and will only become light by flame.

Beauty’s horror is a realization.
We have forgotten again
that beauty is using us (needs us)
for her own discovery.

And, we will forget,
under the weight of our body,
till we surrender to that heaviness
and the light in its bonds.
